Yet we’ve settled into a new reality—where classes, work meetings, doctor’s visits, and happy hours are all virtual. But what are the security implications of our new reality where remote work is the norm? Read on to learn more about how we can help our customers securely enable remote work and have it be part of a more comprehensive security approach.
